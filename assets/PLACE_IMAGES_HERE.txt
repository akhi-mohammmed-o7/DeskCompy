Dress a buddy, or bring your own.

Images here replace the drawn body of a built-in buddy. PNG, WEBP, and JPG all
work. Use a transparent background: the window is clear around the picture.

The eight vector bodies are used wherever a file is missing. A file replaces
the body only. The glow, focus ring, dew drop, speech bubble, mirror particles,
caption, and glimmer bars stay. Idle fidgets, tricks, and accessories are drawn
in vectors and still play around an image.

One folder per buddy (recommended)
----------------------------------

  assets/moth/       Lumen, the lantern moth
  assets/cat/        Mochi, the loaf cat
  assets/frog/       Pip, the pocket frog
  assets/ghost/      Wisp, the desk ghost
  assets/axolotl/    Nori, the pocket axolotl
  assets/dog/        Rex, the desk dog
  assets/bunny/      Pipkin, the bunny
  assets/owl/        Hoot, the owl

Inside a buddy folder:

  companion.png    used for every state that has no file of its own
  calm.png         hanging out
  working.png      studying or working
  scatter.png      gaming
  sleeping.png     five quiet minutes
  focus.png        while the focus timer is running

The old flat layout still works: calm.png, working.png, scatter.png,
sleeping.png, focus.png, and companion.png at the root of assets. A root file
named after a buddy (cat.png, frog.png, ...) is used as that buddy's portrait.

Animated states
---------------

Add numbered frames instead of (or beside) a single picture:

  working_0.png, working_1.png, working_2.png, ...

The sequence plays in order at about 6 frames per second and loops while the
state lasts. A gap in the numbering ends the sequence, and a plain
working.png is used if no numbered frames exist. Keep every frame the same
size; frames taller than 168 px are scaled down to fit, then the whole picture
is fitted into the window. Up to 24 frames are read per state, and a sequence
stops early at a 24-megapixel total.

Files are re-read while the app is open, so you can swap them without
restarting. After packaging, a folder named assets next to DeskCompy.exe
overrides the images bundled in.

Your own buddies
----------------

Open %APPDATA%\DeskCompy\buddies (create it if needed), then restart the
app. Make a folder with a lowercase slug name (bean, my-cat) and put a
buddy.json inside:

  {
    "name": "Bean",
    "species": "desk bean",
    "tagline": "round and supportive",
    "shape": "cat",
    "body": "#f0d0b0",
    "trim": "#6b4d52",
    "accent": "#ffb08c",
    "lines": {"poke": ["Bean has been booped."]}
  }

name is required. shape is one of moth, cat, frog, ghost, axolotl, dog, bunny,
owl; it is the body used until you add art in the same folder (calm.png,
working.png, ...). Lines are optional and can cover: meet, study, game, work,
chill, away, back, poke, late, long, focus_start, focus_done, water, click,
type, work_tired, fun, study_grind, game_hype, smalltalk, trick.

Custom files are local. Nothing is downloaded, and no file in this folder is
ever executed. Manifests are capped at 32 KB; strings are capped and stripped
of invisible control characters; colours must be #rrggbb; images are rejected
over 4 MB, larger than 1024 x 1024, or linked from outside the buddy folder.

What the buddy notices
----------------------

Only rhythm: how fast keys and clicks arrive, and how long the desk has been
quiet. It never reads which keys you press, window titles, or screen contents.
The little flying keycaps are decorative letters chosen at random, not your
input. Activity is a best guess from cadence, so a clicky spreadsheet and a
clicky game can look alike. If the guess is wrong, use Activity in the menu to
pin the right one; the caption marks it "set", and auto resumes when you ask.

Asking the buddy something uses a fixed keyword list and canned replies. The
typed line is cleaned, matched, and dropped: it is not stored, logged, or sent
anywhere.
